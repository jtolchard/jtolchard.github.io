I am a permanent research engineer at the Centre de Résonance Magnétique Nucléaire à Très Hauts Champs (CRMN) in Lyon, France, where I am part of the team that looks after the instrumentation and the helium recovery system, and helps train the researchers who come to use high-field solution-state NMR. As a member of the HMRLab hyperpolarisation group, I also help bring new high-field and dissolution-DNP methods into routine use.

Before Lyon, I spent three years as a biocurator at the Protein Data Bank in Europe (EMBL-EBI), where, alongside my curation work, I helped represent the NMR community on data integrity, deposition and improving the validation of NMR structures. I trained in structural biology, with a PhD at the University of East Anglia and five years as a postdoc in Bordeaux, using solution and solid-state NMR to study membrane proteins, amyloids and bacterial secretion systems. I also write scientific software; some of it is on the Code page.
